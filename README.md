# <p align="center"> <img src="icons/Group 3.png" width="40"> LiminalPoint </p>

<p align="center">Chromium extension that spoofs your browser's fingerprint and blocks ads & trackers </p>

 <p align="center">
<sub><b>#####</b></sub>
</p>


 <p align="center">
Every website you visit builds a silent profile of you. 
Not just cookies, your GPU, your fonts, your screen resolution, the way your browser renders audio. 
Hundreds of signals, stitched together into a fingerprint that follows you everywhere. 
In incognito mode. With a VPN. After clearing your cookies. <b>Always</b>. <br>
</p>

 <p align="center">
<b>LiminalPoint</b> spoofs your browser's identity at API level. <br>
Every time you rotate your identity, you become a completely different machine.<br>
Consistent within a session. Unrecognizable across them.<br>
</p>

<p align="center">
<sub><b>#####</b></sub>
</p>

<p align="center">No cloud. No account. No telemetry. No data leaving your machine. Ever. </p>
<br>

<p align="center">
The extension generates a <b>deterministic profile</b> from random seeds. 
The same seed always produces the same noise, so fingerprinting tests that read Canvas/Audio multiple times will see consistent results, like a real browser.
</p>

<p align="center">
Two buit-in static rule sets block network-level advertising and tracker requests before leaving the browser.
</p>

<pre>4.      Pin the extension -> done!</pre>
